Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+38%

Diversification is accelerating

Automotive and IoT combined grew 38% YoY to $2.54B — Automotive surged 59% to $959M and IoT rose 27% to $1.58B — as Qualcomm's push beyond phones kept paying off.

$6.9B

Handsets still the engine

Handset chips brought in $6.93B, up 12% YoY and 63% of total revenue, inside QCT's $9.47B (+18%). QTL licensing was flat at $1.32B.

$2.7B

Cash back to shareholders

Qualcomm returned $2.7B to shareholders in the quarter — $1.7B of buybacks (11 million shares) plus $938M of dividends — and guided Q3 FY25 revenue to $9.9B–$10.7B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP revenue includes $143M of licensing revenue from a recent settlement of a licensing dispute, not allocated to segment results and excluded from non-GAAP revenue.
  • Operating expenses include $34M of gains from revaluing deferred compensation plan liabilities, offset by $34M of losses on the related plan assets inside investment income — the two cancel out.
  • Non-GAAP results also exclude $703M of share-based compensation and $53M of acquisition-related charges; the tax line includes a $100M benefit from the foreign-derived intangible income deduction tied to capitalized R&D.
