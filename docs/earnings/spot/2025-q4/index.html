Spotify — Q4 2025 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q4 2025 · Quarter ended 2025-12-31
Reported 2026-02-10

Spotify closed 2025 with revenue up 7% to €4,531M (about $5,322M) — 13% in constant currency — a record 33.1% gross margin, and net income of €1,174M, lifted by a €320M net finance gain and a €153M tax benefit.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

33.1%

A record gross margin

Gross margin hit a record 33.1% (€1,499M of gross profit), and operating income of €701M beat the €620M guidance — helped by social charges coming in €67M below forecast as the share price fell.

751M

Record user growth

Monthly users jumped by a record 38M in the quarter to 751M (+11% YoY), and Premium subscribers rose 10% to 290M. The year's Wrapped engaged more than 300M people.

€834M

Cash generation stayed strong

Free cash flow was €834M in the quarter and €2.9B for 2025, taking cash and short-term investments to €9.5B. Net income of €1,174M also got a lift from a €153M tax benefit.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Social charges: operating expenses included a €50M social-charge credit (a negative charge) because Spotify's share price fell in the quarter — €67M below forecast. These share-price-linked charges swing either way and flattered operating income.
  • Tax benefit: the €153M income-tax benefit, instead of a normal tax charge, lifted net income above the €1,021M pre-tax profit.
