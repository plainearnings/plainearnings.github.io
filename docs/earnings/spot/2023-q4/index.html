Spotify — Q4 2023 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q4 2023 · Quarter ended 2023-12-31
Reported 2024-02-06

Spotify grew revenue 16% to €3.67B (about $4.1B) and narrowed its operating loss to €75M as 602 million monthly users and 236 million paying subscribers each grew double digits — but €143M of efficiency charges kept the quarter in the red, with a net loss of €70M.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

602M

Record user growth

Monthly active users grew 23% to 602 million — 28 million net additions in the quarter, the second largest Q4 in Spotify's history — contributing to record full-year net additions of 113 million. Premium subscribers rose 15% to 236 million, with record full-year net additions of 31 million, and ad-supported users grew 28% to 379 million.

€68M

Profitable underneath the charges

The reported operating loss of €75M narrowed from €231M a year ago, but it included €143M of efficiency charges — severance from the December workforce reduction and real estate impairments. Excluding them, adjusted operating income was €68M, more than double Q3 2023. Gross margin hit 26.7%, up 140 bps YoY, on better music and podcast profitability.

€396M

Cash piled up

Free cash flow was €396M, bringing trailing-12-month free cash flow to €678M and the cash pile to €4.3B. Net loss narrowed to €70M from €270M a year ago. Guidance for Q1 2024: 618M users, 239M subscribers, €3.6B revenue, 26.4% gross margin and €180M operating income.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses included €143M of charges from efficiency actions taken late in the quarter — €110M of employee severance and related charges from the December workforce reduction plus a €33M impairment charge on real estate assets. Excluding them, adjusted operating income was €68M, more than double Q3 2023's €32M.
  • Operating expenses included €33M of 'social charges' — Swedish payroll taxes on share-based pay that swing with Spotify's share price — versus €1M a year ago.
  • Gross profit carried €4M of net severance related charges, and the quarter's €143M in efficiency charges included €4M in cost of revenue.
  • The quarter carried a €61M income tax benefit on a €131M pre-tax loss, versus a €21M benefit a year ago.
