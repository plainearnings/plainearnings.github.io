Spotify — Q2 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q2 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-06-30
Reported 2024-07-23

Spotify grew revenue 20% to €3.81B (about $4.1B) and posted a record quarter — gross margin of 29.2% and operating income of €266M, both records — as 626 million monthly users and 246 million paying subscribers each grew double digits, swinging net income to €274M from a €302M loss a year ago.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

29.2%

Record profitability

Gross margin hit a record 29.2%, up 510 bps YoY, on better music and podcast profitability and lower other costs of revenue. Premium gross margin reached 31.4% and Ad-Supported gross margin rose to 13.4%. Operating income hit a record €266M, a 7.0% margin, versus a €247M loss a year ago — Spotify's second straight profitable quarter.

626M

Users kept growing

Monthly active users grew 14% to 626 million — 11 million net additions in the quarter, though 5 million below guidance as marketing was recalibrated — with growth across all regions, led by Rest of World and Europe. Premium subscribers rose 12% to 246 million, 1 million above guidance, and ad-supported users grew 15% to 393 million.

€490M

Cash piled up

Free cash flow hit a record €490M, bringing trailing-12-month free cash flow to €1.3B and the cash pile to €5.4B. Net income swung to €274M from a €302M loss a year ago. Guidance for Q3 2024: 639M users, 251M subscribers, €4.0B revenue, 30.2% gross margin and €405M operating income.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses included €59M of 'social charges' — Swedish payroll taxes on share-based pay that swing with Spotify's share price — versus €25M a year ago, and the charges came in €46M above forecast as the share price rose; €58M of the €59M sat in operating expenses.
  • Finance costs of €72M included a €43M fair-value loss on the Exchangeable Notes as the share price rose, plus an €18M loss on derivative liabilities — largely offset by €52M of interest income and €18M of dividend income, leaving finance items at a net +€4M.
  • The quarter carried a €14M impairment charge on real estate assets, part of the efficiency actions started in late 2023.
  • The quarter carried a €4M income tax benefit on €270M of pre-tax income — a -1.6% effective tax rate — driven by recognizing deferred tax assets on the unrealized gain in Spotify's Tencent Music investment, versus a €61M tax expense a year ago.
