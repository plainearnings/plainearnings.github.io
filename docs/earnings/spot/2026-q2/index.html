Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

300M

Subscribers carry the business

Premium subscribers grew 9% to 300 million — 7 million net adds, ahead of guidance — and Premium revenue rose 15%. Total monthly users reached 777 million, up 12%.

33.4%

Margins keep expanding

Gross margin reached 33.4%, up from 31.5% a year ago, and operating income rose 61% to €655M — ahead of guidance, helped by lower social charges (payroll taxes tied to the share price).

€797M

Cash generation is now routine

Free cash flow was €797M in the quarter and €3.3B over the last twelve months. For Q3 Spotify guided to €5.0B of revenue and €670M of operating income.
