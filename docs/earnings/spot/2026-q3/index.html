Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+17%

Subscriptions carry the business

Premium is 90% of revenue and grew 17% — 306M subscribers, with price rises lifting revenue per user. The free, ad-funded tier is the funnel, not the engine.

33.0%

A record gross margin

Gross margin hit a Q3 record 33.0% — Premium kept more of each euro as revenue grew faster than the music licensing costs it pays out.

€735M

Cash keeps piling up

Free cash flow was €735M in the quarter and the cash pile reached €9.9B — even as operating costs rose 26% on marketing and cloud/AI spending.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses included €22M of 'social charges' — Swedish payroll taxes on share-based pay that swing with Spotify's share price; they came in €13M above forecast.
