Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+14%

Constant-currency growth accelerated

Reported revenue rose 8% to €4,533M, held back by currency moves; in constant currency it grew 14%. Premium subscribers rose 9% to 293M and monthly users reached 761M, both ahead of guidance.

€184M

A notes gain padded the bottom line

Finance income included a €184M fair-value gain on Spotify's Exchangeable Notes, which were then settled and taken off the balance sheet during the quarter. Even without it, operating income rose 40% to €715M on a 33.0% gross margin.

€824M

Cash flow stayed very strong

Free cash flow was €824M in the quarter, taking the last-twelve-months total to €3.2B, while operating cash flow was €836M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Fair-value gain on Exchangeable Notes: €184M inside finance income — a non-cash gain recorded before the notes were settled and derecognized in the quarter (a €1,304M cash settlement).
