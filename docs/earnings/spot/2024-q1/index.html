Spotify — Q1 2024 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q1 2024 · Quarter ended 2024-03-31
Reported 2024-04-23

Spotify grew revenue 20% to €3.64B (about $3.9B) and posted a record quarter — gross margin of 27.6% and operating income of €168M, both records — as 615 million monthly users and 239 million paying subscribers each grew double digits, swinging net income to €197M from a €225M loss a year ago.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

27.6%

Record profitability

Gross margin hit 27.6%, up 243 bps YoY, on better music and podcast profitability and lower other costs of revenue — gross profit passed €1 billion for the first time in Spotify's history. Premium gross margin reached 30.2% and Ad-Supported swung to a 6.4% gross margin from a loss a year ago. Operating income hit a record €168M, a 4.6% margin, versus a €156M loss a year ago.

615M

Users kept growing

Monthly active users grew 19% to 615 million — 13 million net additions in the quarter, though 3 million below guidance as marketing was moderated — with growth across all regions, led by Latin America and Rest of World. Premium subscribers rose 14% to 239 million, in line with guidance, and ad-supported users grew 22% to 388 million.

€207M

Cash piled up

Free cash flow was €207M, bringing trailing-12-month free cash flow to €828M and the cash pile to €4.7B. Net income swung to €197M from a €225M loss a year ago. Guidance for Q2 2024: 631M users, 245M subscribers, €3.8B revenue, 28.1% gross margin and €250M operating income.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating expenses included €82M of 'social charges' — Swedish payroll taxes on share-based pay that swing with Spotify's share price — versus €12M a year ago, and the charges came in €74M above forecast as the share price rose.
  • Finance costs of €53M included a €35M fair-value loss on the Exchangeable Notes as the share price rose, plus an €8M loss on derivative liabilities — largely offset by €45M of interest income, leaving finance items at a net +€6M.
  • The quarter carried a €4M impairment charge on real estate assets, part of the efficiency actions started in late 2023.
  • The quarter carried a €23M income tax benefit on €174M of pre-tax income — a -13.0% effective tax rate — driven by a €64M deferred-tax benefit from the unrealized gain in Spotify's Tencent Music investment, partly offset by €26M of tax expense from using share-based compensation deductions, versus a €19M tax expense a year ago.
