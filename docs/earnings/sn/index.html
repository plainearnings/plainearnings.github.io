Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Fourteenth straight quarter of double-digit growth

Net sales hit $1,976.4M, up 21.2% YoY from $1,630.2M — the 14th consecutive quarter of double-digit growth and the second straight above 20%. Ninja grew 27.7% to $1,083.2M and Shark 14.2% to $893.2M. By category, Cooking and Beverage Appliances rose 36.6% on the Ninja Luxe Café espresso machine and the Ninja Crispi, Beauty and Home Environment rose 45.8%, Food Preparation 18.3% and Cleaning Appliances 4.1%. International sales grew 25.2% and Domestic 19.4%.

59.2%

Margins jumped — mostly on a tariff refund

Gross margin jumped to 59.2% from 50.1% a year ago, helped by $243.8M of IEEPA tariff-refund claims, less $45.2M payable to factory partners. Even stripping those out, Adjusted Gross Margin rose 560 basis points to 55.9%. Operating income rose 68.5% to $442.9M, a 22.4% margin, and Adjusted Operating Income was $425.5M. Net income rose 79.3% to $338.4M, or $2.39 per diluted share; Adjusted Net Income rose 55.1% to $330.9M.

$6.65–$6.75

Outlook raised into the holidays

SharkNinja raised its full-year outlook: net sales now expected up 18.0% to 19.0% (was 16.0% to 17.0%), Adjusted Net Income per diluted share of $6.65 to $6.75, and Adjusted EBITDA of $1,380M to $1,391M. In Q3 it recognized $109.6M of 2025-duty refunds out of a ~$247.1M total claim accepted by U.S. Customs and Border Protection, with about $35.8M more expected in Q4 2026.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • SharkNinja recognized $109.6M of refund claims for IEEPA duties paid in 2025 (of a ~$247.1M total claim accepted by U.S. Customs and Border Protection), less $45.2M payable to factory partners, plus $5.7M of related interest income — a net ~$70.0M benefit to GAAP results that is excluded from Adjusted results because it relates to a prior year. Gross margin benefited from $243.8M of refund claims in total, less $45.2M payable to factory partners.
  • Share-based compensation was $42.0M in Q3, up from $9.1M a year ago — excluded from Adjusted results.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $4.9M in Q3 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Foreign currency losses, net were $4.5M in Q3 — excluded from Adjusted results.
