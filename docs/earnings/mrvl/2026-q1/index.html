Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+76%

Data Center drove the record

Data Center revenue hit $1.44B — 76% of the company — up 76% YoY on custom silicon programs and electro-optics shipments, and total revenue set a record at $1.90B, up 63% YoY.

$178M

Back in the black

GAAP net income was $177.9M versus a $215.6M loss a year ago, as operating income swung to $270.6M from a $152.3M loss. Non-GAAP net income was $540.0M.

$333M

Cash kept flowing

Operating cash flow was $332.9M in the quarter, and Marvell spent $340M buying back its own shares and paid $51.8M in dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • On April 7, 2025 Marvell agreed to sell its automotive ethernet business to Infineon for $2.5B in cash; the business sat as $588.2M of assets held for sale on the balance sheet, and the sale closed August 14, 2025, after this quarter ended, so no gain appears here.
  • GAAP results include $142.1M of stock-based compensation and $245.7M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP — that is why non-GAAP net income ($540.0M) is triple GAAP net income ($177.9M).
  • Restructuring related gains of $12.3M net this quarter — mostly a gain on selling property, partly offset by severance and facility-exit costs.
