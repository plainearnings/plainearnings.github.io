Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+69%

Data Center drove the record

Data Center revenue hit $1.49B — 74% of the company — up 69% YoY on AI demand for custom silicon and electro-optics, and total revenue set a record at $2.01B, up 58% YoY.

$195M

Back in the black

GAAP net income was $194.8M versus a $193.3M loss a year ago, as operating income swung to $290.1M from a $100.4M loss. Non-GAAP net income was $585.5M.

$462M

Cash kept flowing

Operating cash flow was $461.6M in the quarter, and Marvell spent $200M buying back its own shares and paid $51.7M in dividends.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Marvell agreed in April 2025 to sell its automotive ethernet business to Infineon for $2.5B in cash; the sale closed August 14, 2025, after this quarter ended, so no gain appears here — the business sat as $595.5M of assets held for sale on the balance sheet, and the gain lands in Q3 FY26.
  • GAAP results include $153.6M of stock-based compensation and $243.7M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP — that is why non-GAAP net income ($585.5M) is triple GAAP net income ($194.8M).
  • Restructuring related charges of $8.7M this quarter — severance and facility-exit costs, net.
