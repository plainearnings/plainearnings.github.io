Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+92%

Data Center nearly doubled

Data Center revenue hit $880.9M — 69% of the company — up 92% YoY as electro-optics products grew and custom AI programs began to ramp, and total revenue grew 10% sequentially, above the midpoint of guidance.

-$193M

Still a GAAP loss

GAAP net loss was $193.3M, or -$0.22 per share, versus a $207.5M loss a year ago. Operating loss was $100.4M, as R&D and SG&A alone ($684.0M) exceeded gross profit of $587.6M — but non-GAAP net income was $266.2M, or $0.30 per share.

$1.45B

Every market expected to grow next quarter

Marvell guided Q3 FY25 revenue to $1.450B ±5% — 14% sequential growth at the midpoint — with all five end markets growing sequentially and enterprise networking and carrier returning to growth, plus a big jump in operating leverage.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP results include $154.9M of stock-based compensation and $275.7M of amortization of acquired intangible assets, both excluded from non-GAAP — that is why non-GAAP net income ($266.2M) is $459.5M above the GAAP net loss (-$193.3M).
  • Restructuring-related charges were $4.0M this quarter, down from $42.0M a year ago; the big restructuring and impairment charges hit in earlier quarters.
  • Marvell recorded a $47.1M tax provision despite a $146.2M pre-tax loss — the tax line adds to the loss rather than softening it, unlike a year ago when it was a $44.1M benefit.
