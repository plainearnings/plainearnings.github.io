Almost every dollar of Bullish's reported revenue is a digital asset bought and immediately resold — $99.78 of each $100 goes straight back out. The real business is the $84.4M of adjusted revenue. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$598M

The headline profit is mostly a paper gain

A $598.4M non-cash gain from revaluing the digital assets Bullish holds — versus an $11.4M loss a year ago — is bigger than the entire $576.3M net income. Strip out the mark-to-market swings and adjusted net income was $18.7M, up 36%.

+24%

The underlying business is growing

Adjusted revenue rose 10% to $84.4M and adjusted EBITDA rose 24% to $35.6M, a 42% margin, as subscription, services and other revenue grew 13%. Adjusted transaction revenue was $28.0M, up from $26.7M.

$300M

Buyback authorized, Equiniti deal on track

Bullish's board authorized a $300M share repurchase program, and the Equiniti acquisition — the deal that would put Bullish into tokenized equities — is expected to close in January 2027. Digital asset sales fell 23% to $32.1B in a weaker market.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $598.4M non-cash gain from the change in fair value of digital assets held (vs an $11.4M loss in Q3 2025) is the main reason GAAP net income of $576.3M dwarfs adjusted net income of $18.7M.
  • A $96.9M loss on the change in fair value of derivatives (vs $5.5M a year ago) and an $82.7M gain on investments in financial assets (vs $55.0M) also swung with crypto markets and are excluded from Bullish's adjusted figures.
  • Share-based payment expenses were $44.6M in the first nine months of 2026 ($15.6M employee awards plus $29.0M other awards) — a real cost that Bullish's adjusted net income and adjusted EBITDA both set aside.
