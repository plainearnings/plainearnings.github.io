Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Product growth held at 30%

Product revenue rose 30% to $1,227M and total revenue to $1,284M. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have committed to — jumped 42% to $9.77B, and net revenue retention was 125%, meaning existing customers spent a quarter more than a year earlier.

-$310M

Still a GAAP loss, by design of pay

The GAAP net loss was $310M, narrower than $326M a year ago. Snowflake pays staff heavily in shares, which counts as a GAAP expense: on the company's non-GAAP basis, operating income was a profit of $139M.

$782M

Cash flow is the counterweight

Operating cash flow was $781M and adjusted free cash flow $782M — 61% of revenue — in the seasonally strong collection quarter. Snowflake added 740 net new customers, up 40% YoY, and 733 customers now spend over $1M a year.
