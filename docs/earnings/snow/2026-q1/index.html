Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+26%

Product growth stayed strong

Product revenue rose 26% to $997M, the net revenue retention rate was 124%, and remaining performance obligations — work customers have already contracted — grew 34% to $6.7B. Snowflake also guided Q2 product revenue to $1,035–$1,040M, up 25%, and full-year FY26 product revenue to $4,325M, up 25%.

-$447M

Still a GAAP loss, mostly stock pay and an office exit

The GAAP operating loss was $447M. The main reasons: $409M of stock-based compensation-related charges and a $106M impairment for exiting the San Mateo office. Leaving those and similar items out, non-GAAP operating income was $92M, a 9% margin.

606

Big customers kept spending

Snowflake ended the quarter with 606 customers spending over $1M a year on the platform (+27% YoY) and 754 Forbes Global 2000 customers. Operating cash flow was $228M and adjusted free cash flow was $206M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $106.5M impairment charge for exiting Snowflake's San Mateo office — the office was ceased using during the quarter — booked inside general and administrative expense and excluded from non-GAAP results along with stock-based pay and other items.
