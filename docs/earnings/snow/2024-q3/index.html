Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Product growth stayed strong

Product revenue rose 34% to $698.5M, the net revenue retention rate was 135%, and remaining performance obligations — work customers have already contracted — grew 23% to $3.7B. Snowflake guided Q4 FY24 product revenue to $716–$721M, up 29–30%, at a 4% non-GAAP operating margin, and full-year FY24 product revenue to $2,650M, up 37%.

-$260.6M

Still a GAAP loss, mostly stock pay

The GAAP operating loss was $260.6M, wider than the $206.0M loss a year ago. The main reason: $307.4M of stock-based compensation-related charges, including about $6.2M of employer payroll taxes on employee stock transactions. Leaving those and similar items out, non-GAAP operating income was $71.9M, a 10% margin (up from 8% a year ago).

436

Big customers kept spending

Snowflake ended the quarter with 436 customers spending over $1M a year on the platform (+52% YoY) and 647 Forbes Global 2000 customers (+10% YoY). Operating cash flow was $120.9M, up 53% YoY, and adjusted free cash flow was $110.8M, up 70% YoY.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $307.4M of stock-based compensation-related charges (including about $6.2M of employer payroll taxes on employee stock transactions), excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • $22.5M of amortization of acquired intangibles, also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • $2.7M of expenses associated with acquisitions and strategic investments, also excluded from non-GAAP results.
