Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+37%

Product growth is speeding up

Product revenue rose 37% to $1.49B — the third straight quarter of faster growth — and Snowflake raised full-year product revenue guidance to $6.07B, up 36%.

-$263M

Still a GAAP loss, mostly stock pay

The GAAP operating loss was $263M. The main reason: $456M of stock-based compensation (paying staff in shares) counted as a cost this quarter. Leaving that and similar items out, non-GAAP operating income was $237M.

$9.0B

Customers keep committing more

Remaining performance obligations — revenue customers have contracted but not yet used — hit $9.0B, up 30%, and 828 customers each spent over $1M in the past year, up 27%. Free cash flow was $84M.
