Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Product growth stayed strong

Product revenue rose 34% to $789.6M, the net revenue retention rate was 128%, and remaining performance obligations — work customers have already contracted — grew 46% to $5.0B. Snowflake guided Q2 FY25 product revenue to $805–$810M, up 26–27%, at a 3% non-GAAP operating margin.

-$348.6M

Still a GAAP loss, mostly stock pay

The GAAP operating loss was $348.6M, wider than the $273.2M loss a year ago. The main reason: $360.4M of stock-based compensation-related charges, including about $21.9M of employer payroll taxes on employee stock transactions. Leaving those and similar items out, non-GAAP operating income was $36.2M, a 4% margin (down from 5% a year ago).

485

Big customers kept spending

Snowflake ended the quarter with 485 customers spending over $1M a year on the platform (+30% YoY) and 709 Forbes Global 2000 customers (+8% YoY). Operating cash flow was $355.5M and adjusted free cash flow was $365.7M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $360.4M of stock-based compensation-related charges (including about $21.9M of employer payroll taxes on employee stock transactions), excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • $23.4M of amortization of acquired intangibles, also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • $1.0M of expenses associated with acquisitions and strategic investments, also excluded from non-GAAP results.
