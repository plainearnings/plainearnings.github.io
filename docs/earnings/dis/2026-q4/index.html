Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

~$1.63B

A genuinely strong quarter — with a calendar asterisk

Revenue +16%, segment operating income +43% and adjusted EPS +59% all beat guidance. But the 53rd week supplied ~$1.63B of revenue and ~$490M of operating income; without it, growth is still solid (segment operating income ~+10% for the year) but not spectacular. The Q4 FY27 comparison will lap that extra week.

+92%

Streaming finally pays, parks still rule

Entertainment SVOD operating income more than doubled to $824M, and the segment nearly doubled overall — the streaming turnaround is real. Even so, Experiences produced $2.25B of the $4.97B segment profit, with domestic parks operating income up 36% on record guest numbers and two new cruise ships filling up.

$243M

The portfolio keeps getting more complicated

The NFL deal (10% of ESPN to the NFL) and the Fubo combination (Hulu Live TV merged in, ~70% owned) both boosted revenue this quarter — and both show up as bigger noncontrolling-interest deductions ($243M, nearly double last year) and bigger eliminations. The A+E sale brought in ~$1.3B of cash. FY27 guidance is confident (double-digit adjusted EPS, ~$11B free cash flow, ~$9B buybacks), but Q1 segment operating income is guided to ~$5.4B with growth weighted to the first half.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • 53rd week (not a charge, but a one-off boost): ~$1.63B revenue / ~$490M segment operating income in Q4; ~$0.17 of full-year adjusted EPS. Every year-over-year growth rate in this release is flattered by it.
  • Restructuring and impairment charges were $317M pre-tax ($0.13/share) — excluded from adjusted EPS, as is acquisition amortization of $337M ($0.13/share). Together they explain the entire $0.26 gap between GAAP EPS ($1.51) and adjusted EPS ($1.77).
  • A+E sale (~$1.3B cash, Sep 30, 2026): a portfolio sale, not operating income; it sits in investing cash flow, not in the quarter's earnings.
  • Fubo and NFL Transactions: both closed during fiscal 2026 and are now inside the consolidated results — they add revenue and operating income, but also minority-interest deductions and inter-segment eliminations, so the "growth" they contribute isn't clean organic growth. The release attributes ~2 ppts of revenue growth and ~1 ppt of segment operating income growth to them.
  • Fiscal 2025 tax benefit: the full-year GAAP EPS decline (−18%) is an artifact of a $1,428M income-tax benefit booked in fiscal 2025, not of weaker fiscal 2026 operations — pre-tax income actually rose 18% for the year.
