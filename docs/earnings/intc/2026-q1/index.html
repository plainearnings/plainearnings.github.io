Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Data center is carrying growth

Data Center and AI revenue rose 22% to $5,052M on higher server prices and demand, while client computing was roughly flat at $7,727M. Total revenue rose 7% to $13,577M and gross margin improved to 39.4% from 36.9%.

$3.9B

A goodwill impairment drove the loss

Restructuring and other charges were $4,070M, of which $3,965M was asset impairments — including about $3.9B of non-cash goodwill impairment — versus just $156M of total charges a year ago. Excluding one-offs, non-GAAP EPS was a profit of $0.29.

$1.1B

Cash held up, guidance rose

Intel generated $1,096M of operating cash flow despite the paper charges, repurchased the 49% minority stake in its Fab 34 Ireland venture, and guided Q2 revenue to $13.8B–$14.8B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Asset impairment charges: $3,965M inside restructuring — including about $3.9B of non-cash goodwill impairment — plus $74M of severance and $31M of litigation and other charges.
  • Mark-to-market loss on Escrowed Shares: $1,090M inside 'interest and other, net' — a non-cash charge linked to the U.S. government CHIPS Act share agreement; Intel excludes it from non-GAAP results.
