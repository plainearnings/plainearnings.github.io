Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$5.5B

A $4.1B profit built on a one-time sale

Intel's GAAP net income attributable to Intel was $4,063M, but it includes a $5,452M pre-tax gain from selling 51% of Altera, partly offset by a $1,687M mark-to-market loss on shares escrowed for the U.S. government. Strip those out and non-GAAP net income was $1,023M, or $0.23 a share.

+5%

PC chips carried the quarter

Client Computing revenue rose 5% to $8,535M and earned $2,694M — the PC business is still Intel's profit engine. Data Center and AI slipped 1% to $4,117M, but its operating income more than doubled to $964M from $381M as costs fell.

-$2.3B

Foundry still loses money — and mostly sells to Intel

Intel Foundry booked $4,235M of revenue but lost $2,321M, and only $32M came from outside customers. Intel also took in $5.7B from the U.S. government plus $5.0B from NVIDIA and $2.0B from SoftBank — but the accounting for the government deal is still under SEC review, so these results could be revised.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $5,452M pre-tax gain on selling a 51% stake in Altera, which closed September 12, 2025 and deconsolidated Altera from Intel's results; Intel kept a 49% stake now held as an equity investment. The gain sits in interest and other, net and is excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $1,687M mark-to-market loss on the obligation to issue Escrowed Shares — Intel shares held in escrow for the U.S. Department of Commerce under the CHIPS Act Secure Enclave agreement — also inside interest and other, net; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Gains on equity investments, net were a $221M gain, mostly mark-to-market moves on Intel's investment portfolio rather than chip sales; also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Restructuring and other charges were $175M, versus $5,622M a year ago — the year-ago charge was dominated by impairments, which is why Q3 2024 showed a $9,057M operating loss and a $16,639M net loss attributable to Intel.
  • Intel flags these results as preliminary: the accounting for its U.S. Government transactions is under consultation with SEC staff (delayed by the government shutdown), and if the staff disagrees, Intel may revise Q3 results — materially.
