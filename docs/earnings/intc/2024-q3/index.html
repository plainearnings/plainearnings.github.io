Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$(17.0)B

The charges, not the chips, made the loss

Revenue fell 6% to $13.3B and gross margin collapsed to 15.0% from 42.5% a year ago after $3.1B of manufacturing-asset writedowns landed in cost of sales. The operating loss was $9,057M; excluding the $5.6B of restructuring and impairment charges, the non-GAAP operating loss was $2,369M and non-GAAP EPS was $(0.46) versus GAAP EPS of $(3.88).

$(5.8)B

Foundry lost far more than the whole company earned

Intel Foundry lost $5,844M on $4,352M of segment revenue, and only $67M of that revenue came from outside customers — down 79% YoY — the rest was wafers sold to Intel's own product groups. The product groups still earned $3,337M combined: Client Computing $2,722M, Data Center and AI $347M and Network and Edge $268M.

$(2.7)B

Cash burned while the reset takes hold

Operating cash flow was $4,054M, but after $6,756M of net spending on property, plant and equipment, adjusted free cash flow was $(2,702)M. Intel paid $0.5B in dividends and cut 16,500 jobs under its $10B cost-reduction plan. Q4 guidance is $13.3–14.3B of revenue with GAAP EPS of $(0.24).

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $5,622M of restructuring and other charges under Intel's 2024 Restructuring Plan — $2,193M of employee severance and benefits, $3,393M of asset impairments (including a $2,800M goodwill writedown, mostly Mobileye, and a $108M acquired-intangibles writedown) and $36M of litigation and other; excluded from non-GAAP results. A year ago the same line was $816M.
  • $3.1 billion of non-cash impairments and accelerated depreciation for certain manufacturing assets, mostly the Intel 7 process node — $2.1 billion of impairments and $945 million of accelerated depreciation, substantially all booked in cost of sales; it cut GAAP and non-GAAP results alike. Intel says the charges reflect excess Intel 7 capacity versus projected demand.
  • $9,925M non-cash valuation allowance against US deferred tax assets — recorded because Intel's GAAP results showed a three-year cumulative loss — drove the GAAP tax provision to $7,903M on a pre-tax loss; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $(159)M loss on equity investments, net, versus a $(191)M loss a year ago — mark-to-market moves on Intel's investment portfolio rather than chip sales; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $39M gain from divestiture — deferred consideration on the NAND sale to SK hynix; excluded from non-GAAP results.
