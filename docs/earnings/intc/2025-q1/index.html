Intel — Q1 2025 Earnings Explained simply: where the money came from, and where it went

Q1 2025 · Quarter ended 2025-03-29
Reported 2025-04-24

Intel lost $887 million in the first quarter as flat revenue of $12.7 billion met a thinner 36.9% gross margin, and its chip factories alone lost $2.3 billion while a new CEO moved to cut costs and spending.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

-$887M

Cost cuts weren't enough to save the quarter

Revenue was flat at $12.7B and R&D plus MG&A fell 19% year over year to $4.8B, but gross margin slid to 36.9% from 41.0%, so Intel still lost money on operations. GAAP EPS was $(0.19); non-GAAP EPS was $0.13.

-$2.3B

Foundry lost far more than the whole company

Intel Foundry lost $2,320M on $4,667M of segment revenue, and only $31M of that revenue came from outside customers — the rest was wafers sold to Intel's own product groups. Client Computing still earned $2,361M and Data Center and AI earned $575M.

$(3.7)B

Cash burned while the new CEO resets the plan

Operating cash flow was $813M, but after $6.2B of gross capital spending, adjusted free cash flow was $(3,680)M. New CEO Lip-Bu Tan cut the 2025 operating-expense target to ~$17B (and ~$16B for 2026) and gross capex to $18B; Q2 guidance is $11.2–12.4B of revenue with GAAP EPS of $(0.32). Intel also agreed to sell 51% of Altera to Silver Lake.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $156M of restructuring and other charges under Intel's 2024 Restructuring Plan — $142M of severance, $11M of litigation charges and $3M of asset impairments; excluded from non-GAAP results. Cumulative plan cost reached $2.9B of an expected ~$3.0B total.
  • A $(112)M loss on equity investments, net, versus a $205M gain a year ago — mark-to-market moves on Intel's investment portfolio rather than chip sales, a $317M year-over-year swing.
  • A $94M loss from divestiture: the final close of the NAND sale to SK hynix brought in less than the receivable recorded at the first closing, after a final negotiated settlement; excluded from non-GAAP results.
