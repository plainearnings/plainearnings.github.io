Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$412M

Profitable operations, unprofitable quarter

Revenue fell 7% to $14.3B and gross margin slid to 39.2% from 45.7% a year ago, but Intel still earned $412M from operations after cutting R&D and MG&A 9% to $5.1B. A $752M tax bill on $599M of pre-tax income — a 125.5% rate — pushed the bottom line to a $153M net loss. GAAP EPS was $(0.03); non-GAAP EPS was $0.13.

-$2.3B

Foundry lost far more than the whole company

Intel Foundry lost $2,260M on $4,502M of segment revenue, and only $214M of that revenue came from outside customers — the rest was wafers sold to Intel's own product groups. Client Computing still earned $3,056M, Data Center and AI earned $233M and Network and Edge earned $340M.

$(1.5)B

Cash burned while the new plan takes shape

Operating cash flow was $3,165M, but after $4,667M of net spending on property, plant and equipment, adjusted free cash flow was $(1,503)M. Q1 2025 guidance is $11.7–12.7B of revenue with GAAP EPS of $(0.27). Intel signed its CHIPS Act award for up to $7.86B and received $1.1B in the quarter; headcount fell to 108.9K from 124.8K a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $57M of restructuring and other charges under Intel's 2024 Restructuring Plan — a small quarter after $6,970M for the full year, most of it booked in Q3; excluded from non-GAAP results. A year ago the same line was a $(1,142)M credit, mostly a benefit from VLSI litigation developments, which is why total operating expenses rose even as R&D and MG&A fell.
  • A $316M gain on equity investments, net, versus an $86M gain a year ago — mark-to-market moves on Intel's investment portfolio rather than chip sales; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • $560M of interest received on the refunded European Commission fine (paid in 2009, annulled, refunded in 2022) — a one-off receipt inside interest and other, net; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $39M loss from divestiture — deferred consideration on the NAND sale to SK hynix; excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • For the full year, a $9,925M non-cash valuation allowance against US deferred tax assets — recorded because Intel's GAAP results showed a cumulative loss — drove the full-year GAAP tax rate to 71.6% and the $18,756M net loss attributable to Intel. None of the allowance was recorded in Q4 itself; Intel excludes it from non-GAAP results.
