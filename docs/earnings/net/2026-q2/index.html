Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+36%

Growth re-accelerated

Revenue hit $696.1M, up 36% YoY, with current remaining performance obligations up 35%. Management pointed to record growth in paying customers, large customers and developers on the platform.

$151M

A restructuring charge drove the loss

The $150.7M restructuring and other charge — zero a year ago — is the single biggest reason the GAAP operating loss widened from $67M to $206M. Excluding it and stock pay, non-GAAP operating income was $96.1M, a 13.8% margin.

$141M

Stock pay remains the steady gap

Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were $140.6M in the quarter, about 20% of revenue. Free cash flow was still positive at $56.4M, and Cloudflare held $4,162.8M of cash and available-for-sale securities.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges of $150.693M were taken in the quarter (none in the year-ago quarter); Cloudflare excludes the charge from its non-GAAP results.
  • General and administrative costs include a $1M legal reserve and settlements charge and $2.047M of acquisition-related expenses.
