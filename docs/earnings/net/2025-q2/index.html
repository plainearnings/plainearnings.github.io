Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

Growth reaccelerated past $2B annualized

Revenue hit $512.3M, up 28% YoY from $401.0M a year ago, reaccelerating as Cloudflare crossed $2 billion in annualized revenue. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — grew 39% YoY to $1,976.7M, with current RPO up 33%.

-$50.4M

A bigger GAAP loss, cushioned by interest income

GAAP loss from operations widened to $67.3M, or 13.1% of revenue, versus a $34.7M loss a year ago, as operating expenses grew 30% — faster than revenue. But $20.0M of non-operating income, mostly $25.4M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $50.4M versus $15.1M a year ago. Excluding $132.0M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $72.3M. Cloudflare also raised $2.0B in new 2030 convertible notes during the quarter, lifting cash and securities to $3,959.7M.

114%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 114%, meaning existing paying customers spent 14% more than they did a year earlier. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 3,712 from 3,046, and paying customers overall grew 27% to 265,929.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $131.993M in Q2 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $72.315M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $3.746M in Q2 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $3.840M lease impairment charge landed in general and administrative expenses in Q2 — excluded from non-GAAP results. No one-time compensation charge landed in Q2 2025: the $15.000M one-time compensation charge in the six-month figures dates to the first quarter of 2024.
