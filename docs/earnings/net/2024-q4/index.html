Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Growth held at 27% with record large-customer wins

Revenue hit $459.9M, up 27% YoY from $362.5M a year ago. Customers spending more than $1 million a year grew 47% to 173 — Cloudflare added 55 of them in 2024, more than half in the fourth quarter alone. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $1,686.5M, with 70% expected within 12 months.

-$12.8M

A narrower operating loss, still GAAP-unprofitable

GAAP loss from operations narrowed to $34.7M, or 7.5% of revenue, versus a $42.8M loss a year ago, as operating expenses grew 20% — slower than revenue. $23.9M of non-operating income, mostly $22.0M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $12.8M versus $27.9M a year ago. Excluding $98.4M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $67.2M. Free cash flow was $47.8M, 10% of revenue, down slightly from $50.7M a year ago as Cloudflare spent more on network equipment.

111%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 111%, meaning existing paying customers spent 11% more than they did a year earlier — down from 115% a year ago. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 3,497 from 2,756, and paying customers overall grew 25% to 237,714.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $98.422M in Q4 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $67.247M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $3.082M in Q4 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Acquisition-related and other expenses were $0.462M in Q4 — excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • No one-time compensation charge landed in Q4 2024: the $15.000M one-time compensation charge in the full-year figures dates to the first quarter of 2024.
