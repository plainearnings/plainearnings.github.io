Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+32%

Revenue grew 32% and set records for new business

Revenue hit $362.5M, up 32% YoY from $274.7M, with record new annual contract value — the highest quarterly growth since 2021 — and both the largest new customer win and the largest customer renewal ever. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 2,756 from 2,042, and paying customers overall grew 17% to 189,791. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $1,244.6M, with 73% expected within 12 months.

-$27.9M

Still GAAP-unprofitable, but the loss narrowed

GAAP loss from operations narrowed to $42.8M, or 11.8% of revenue, versus a $50.7M loss a year ago, as sales and marketing grew 25% while revenue grew 32%. $17.0M of non-operating income, mostly $20.2M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $27.9M versus $45.9M a year ago. Excluding $77.3M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $39.8M, an 11.0% margin, and non-GAAP net income was $53.5M. Operating cash flow was $85.4M, 24% of revenue, and free cash flow a Q4 record $50.7M.

115%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 115%, meaning existing paying customers spent 15% more than they did a year earlier — down from 122% a year ago. Paying customers overall grew 17% to 189,791, and large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 2,756 from 2,042.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $77.304M in Q4 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $39.814M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $5.339M in Q4 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • No loss on extinguishing convertible debt landed in Q4 2023: the $50.300M debt-extinguishment loss hit Q2 2023, when Cloudflare repurchased 2025 Notes, and is not part of this quarter's story.
