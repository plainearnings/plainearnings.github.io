Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Growth held at 30% and crossed $1.5B annualized

Revenue hit $378.6M, up 30% YoY from $290.2M a year ago, and annualized revenue crossed $1.5 billion. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 2,878 from 2,156, and paying customers overall grew 17% to 197,138, with a record number of net-new customers spending over $100,000, $500,000, and $1 million a year. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $1,342.8M, with 70% expected within 12 months.

-$35.5M

A wider operating loss, still GAAP-unprofitable

GAAP loss from operations widened to $54.6M, or 14.4% of revenue, versus a $47.3M loss a year ago, as sales and marketing jumped 42% — including a $15.0M one-time compensation charge. $21.3M of non-operating income, mostly $21.3M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $35.5M versus $38.1M a year ago. Excluding $76.7M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $42.4M, an 11.2% margin. Operating cash flow was $73.6M, 19% of revenue.

115%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 115%, meaning existing paying customers spent 15% more than they did a year earlier — down from 117% a year ago. Paying customers overall grew 17% to 197,138, and large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 2,878 from 2,156.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $76.727M in Q1 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $42.443M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $5.266M in Q1 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • A $15.000M one-time compensation charge landed in sales and marketing in Q1 2024 — excluded from non-GAAP results; it is the same charge the Q2 report notes as dating to the first quarter.
  • No loss on extinguishing convertible debt landed in Q1 2024: the $50.300M debt-extinguishment loss that hit Q2 2023's net loss is not part of this quarter's story.
