Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+39%

Growth accelerated to a near-four-year high

Revenue hit $782.5M, up 39% YoY. Management called it Cloudflare's best quarter ever and its fastest revenue growth in nearly four years, with current RPO up 37% YoY.

-$68M

GAAP operating loss widened

GAAP loss from operations was $67.9M, or 8.7% of revenue, versus a $37.5M loss a year ago. Stock-based compensation of $174.1M sits inside the GAAP costs; excluding it and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $137.1M.

$82M

Cash flow stayed positive despite the loss

Net cash from operations was $215.4M and free cash flow (operations less equipment purchases and capitalized software) was $82.1M, or 10% of revenue. Cash, equivalents and available-for-sale securities were $5,128.7M at September 30.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Restructuring and other charges were $11.018M in Q3 ($161.711M in the first nine months) — excluded from Cloudflare's non-GAAP results.
  • A legal reserve and settlements charge of $10.667M sits inside general and administrative expense in Q3 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $174.103M in Q3 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows a loss while non-GAAP operating income was $137.084M.
