Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+30%

Growth held at 30% and crossed $1.6B annualized

Revenue hit $401.0M, up 30% YoY from $308.5M a year ago, and annualized revenue crossed $1.6 billion. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 3,046 from 2,352, and paying customers overall grew 21% to 210,166. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $1,421.0M, with 69% expected within 12 months.

-$15.1M

A narrower operating loss, still GAAP-unprofitable

GAAP loss from operations narrowed to $34.7M, or 8.7% of revenue, versus a $56.2M loss a year ago, as operating expenses grew 20% — slower than revenue. $20.8M of non-operating income, mostly $21.7M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $15.1M versus $94.5M a year ago. Excluding $89.5M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $57.0M, a 14.2% margin. Operating cash flow was $74.8M, 19% of revenue.

112%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 112%, meaning existing paying customers spent 12% more than they did a year earlier — down from 115% a year ago. Paying customers overall grew 21% to 210,166, and large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 3,046 from 2,352.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $89.507M in Q2 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $56.953M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $1.982M in Q2 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Acquisition-related and other expenses were $0.162M in Q2 — excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • No one-time compensation charge landed in Q2 2024: the $15.000M one-time compensation charge in the six-month figures dates to the first quarter of 2024.
  • A year ago, Q2 2023's net loss included a $50.300M loss on extinguishing convertible debt — most of the year-over-year improvement in the net loss is that charge not repeating.
