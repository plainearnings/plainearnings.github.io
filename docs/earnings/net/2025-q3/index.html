Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Growth accelerated for a second straight quarter

Revenue hit $562.0M, up 31% YoY from $430.1M a year ago. Management said growth accelerated for the second consecutive quarter, and remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — grew 43% YoY, with current RPO up 30%.

-$1.3M

Nearly breakeven despite a GAAP operating loss

GAAP loss from operations was $37.5M, or 6.7% of revenue, versus a $30.8M loss a year ago. But $39.3M of non-operating income — mostly $42.5M of interest income — nearly wiped it out, leaving a net loss of just $1.3M versus $15.3M a year ago. Excluding stock-based compensation of $119.7M and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $85.9M.

119%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 119%, up from 110% a year ago, meaning existing paying customers spent 19% more than they did a year earlier. Large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 4,009 from 3,265, and paying customers overall grew 33%.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $119.699M in Q3 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $85.938M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $3.699M in Q3 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • No one-time charge landed in Q3 itself: the $3.840M lease impairment and the $15.000M one-time compensation charge in the nine-month figures fall outside this quarter.
