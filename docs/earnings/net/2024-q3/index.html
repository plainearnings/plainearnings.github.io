Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+28%

Growth held at 28% with a record large-customer quarter

Revenue hit $430.1M, up 28% YoY from $335.6M a year ago. Cloudflare added a record 219 large customers in the quarter, taking the total paying over $100,000 a year to 3,265, and 35% of the Fortune 500 are now paying customers. Remaining performance obligations — work customers have already committed to — stood at $1,503.4M, with 70% expected within 12 months.

-$15.3M

A narrower operating loss, still GAAP-unprofitable

GAAP loss from operations narrowed to $30.8M, or 7.2% of revenue, versus a $39.2M loss a year ago, as operating expenses grew 23% — slower than revenue. $18.0M of non-operating income, mostly $22.5M of interest income, narrowed the net loss to $15.3M versus $23.5M a year ago. Excluding $91.8M of stock-based compensation and other items, non-GAAP operating income was $63.5M, a 14.8% margin. Operating cash flow was $104.7M, 24% of revenue.

110%

Existing customers keep spending more

Dollar-based net retention was 110%, meaning existing paying customers spent 10% more than they did a year earlier — down from 116% a year ago. Paying customers overall grew 22% to 221,540, and large customers — those paying over $100,000 a year — rose to 3,265 from 2,558.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Stock-based compensation and related employer payroll taxes totaled $91.767M in Q3 across cost of revenue and operating expenses — the main reason GAAP shows an operating loss while non-GAAP operating income was $63.468M.
  • Amortization of acquired intangible assets was $2.417M in Q3 — also excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Acquisition-related and other expenses were $0.078M in Q3 — excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • No one-time compensation charge landed in Q3 2024: the $15.000M one-time compensation charge in the nine-month figures dates to the first quarter of 2024.
