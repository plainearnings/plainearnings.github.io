Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Growth re-accelerated into year-end

Revenue rose 34% to $615M. Cloudflare closed its largest annual-contract-value deal ever (about $42.5M a year), total new ACV grew nearly 50%, and remaining performance obligations grew 48%.

$90M

Non-GAAP profit vs GAAP loss

Non-GAAP operating income was $90M, a 15% margin. The GAAP operating loss of $49M is after $132M of stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes in the quarter.

$99M

Cash flow doubled

Free cash flow was $99M, 16% of revenue, up from $48M a year ago; Cloudflare ended 2025 with $4,101M of cash and available-for-sale securities.
