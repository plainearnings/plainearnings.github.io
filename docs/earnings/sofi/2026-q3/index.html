Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+66%

Lending is the engine

Lending segment net revenue jumped 66% to $821M. Net interest income rose 57%, loan origination fees rose 158% on personal loan growth, and the Loan Platform Business originated $3.8B of loans for third parties, adding $153M to adjusted net revenue.

17.1M

Members are arriving in record numbers

SoFi added a record 1.2 million members in the quarter, taking the total to 17.1 million (+35% YoY). Deposits grew $5.8B in the quarter to $51.3B, and Financial Services products rose 44% to 23.2 million.

$162M

Crypto is huge and nearly profitless

Crypto transaction revenue of $162M cost $160M to deliver, netting just $1.6M — the same pattern as Block's bitcoin. Technology Platform revenue also fell 24% after a large client left, though it grew 18% excluding that client.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • GAAP net revenue of $1,385M includes a $14M non-cash change in the value of loan servicing rights; SoFi's adjusted net revenue of $1,371M excludes it. Adjusted net income was $243M vs GAAP net income of $241M.
