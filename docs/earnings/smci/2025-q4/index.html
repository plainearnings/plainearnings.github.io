Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+7.5%

Sales grew, but profit shrank by a third

Net sales hit $5,757M, up from $5,355M a year ago, yet net income fell 34% to $195M from $297M. Gross margin narrowed to 9.5% from 10.2% and operating costs rose 23%, so the extra sales did not reach the bottom line.

9.5%

Thin margins are the story

Cost of sales ate $5,213M of the $5,757M in sales, leaving a 9.5% gross margin — down from 10.2% a year ago and 9.6% last quarter. Operating income fell 21% to $228M as R&D jumped 44% YoY to $183M on hiring for next-generation server and rack designs.

$864M

Cash came back strongly

Operations generated $864M of cash in the quarter, against $79M of capital expenditures and investments. Cash ended June 30 at $5.2B versus $4.8B of bank debt and convertible notes. For the full year, sales grew 47% to $22.0B while net income fell to $1.0B from $1.2B.
