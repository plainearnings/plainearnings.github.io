Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+180.1%

Sales nearly tripled on AI servers

Net sales hit $5,937M, up from $2,120M a year ago, driven by demand for GPU servers, high-performance computing and rack-scale solutions. Server and storage systems alone were $5,748M, up 192% year over year.

$0.67

Profit more than doubled

Net income was $424M, up 170% from $157M a year ago, and GAAP diluted EPS was $0.67 versus $0.27. Operating income nearly tripled to $509M even as gross margin narrowed to 13.1% from 16.7% a year ago.

Feb 25, 2025

Five months late, after the auditor quit

These final figures landed in the 10-Q on February 25, 2025 — the quarter had only been reported as preliminary ranges on November 5, 2024, after Ernst & Young resigned on October 24, 2024 and a special committee reviewed governance concerns. The committee found no evidence of misconduct, and BDO USA is the new auditor.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Delayed filing: these final figures come from the Form 10-Q filed February 25, 2025 — nearly five months after quarter-end — after EY resigned as auditor on October 24, 2024 and an independent special committee completed its review on December 2, 2024. The quarter was first reported only as preliminary ranges on November 5, 2024 (net sales $5.9–6.0B, non-GAAP diluted EPS $0.75–0.76).
  • Stock-based compensation was approximately $64.0M in the quarter, per the 10-Q cash flow statement; the November 5, 2024 preliminary release put non-GAAP diluted EPS at $0.75–0.76 versus GAAP $0.67, with the gap mostly stock-based compensation.
