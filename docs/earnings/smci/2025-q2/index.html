Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+54.9%

Sales jumped, margins thinned

Net sales hit $5,678M, up from $3,665M a year ago, but gross margin narrowed to 11.8% from 15.4% as hardware costs ate $5,008M of the total. Operating income was flat at $369M versus $371M a year ago.

$0.51

Profit barely grew

Net income was $321M, up 8% from $296M a year ago, and GAAP diluted EPS was $0.51 — flat year over year. Operating expenses rose 56% to $301M, with R&D up 45%, sales and marketing up 70% and G&A up 71%.

$23.5–25.0B

Preliminary numbers, delayed filings, guidance cut

These figures were labeled preliminary — Supermicro's audited 10-K and 10-Qs were still unfiled when it reported, and the final 10-Q landed February 25, 2025. It also cut full-year FY25 revenue guidance to $23.5–25.0B from $26–30B, and disclosed DOJ and SEC subpoenas following a short-seller report.
