Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Checkout volume keeps compounding

GMV grew 34% to $14.4B — the 12th straight quarter above 30%. Direct-to-consumer volume grew 55% to $5.1B, with Affirm Card GMV up 117% to $3.3B. Active consumers rose 19% to 28.7 million and active merchants 48% to 621,000.

$617M

Profit is scaling faster than revenue

Revenue less transaction costs rose 36% to $617M, or 4.3% of GMV. Operating income nearly tripled to $187M — a 14.8% margin vs 6.8% a year ago — and adjusted operating income reached $380M, a 30% margin.

2.9%

Credit costs are the watch item

30+ day delinquencies (ex-Peloton and Pay in 4) ticked up 9 basis points YoY to 2.9%, and the provision for credit losses rose 59% to $259M — faster than revenue — as the loan book grew. Recent loan vintages are tracking toward about 3.5% ultimate net charge-offs, in line with expectations.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Operating income includes $51M of enterprise warrant and share-based expense tied to two big partners, inside sales and marketing — the main reason GAAP operating income ($187M) is about half the adjusted figure ($380M, which also adds back $86M of depreciation and amortization and $56M of stock-based compensation).
