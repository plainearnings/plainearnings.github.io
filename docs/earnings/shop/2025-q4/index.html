Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

A record holiday quarter

Revenue rose 31% to $3,672M and merchants sold a record $123.8B through the platform (GMV, also +31%). Full-year revenue grew 30% to $11.6B.

$631M

Operating profit kept compounding

Operating income rose 36% to $631M, and free cash flow was $715M — a 19% margin, the tenth straight quarter of double-digit free-cash-flow margins. Shopify also launched a $2B share repurchase program.

$743M

Investment gains helped, but were smaller than last year

Net income was $743M, down from $1,293M a year ago — that quarter's investment gains were far larger ($906M vs $182M this quarter). Excluding equity-investment gains, net income was $594M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Convertible-note derivative: a $94M loss on the embedded derivative held to settle Shopify's convertible senior notes, which were settled in the quarter (the full-year loss was $123M).
  • Equity investments: a $248M net gain on equity and other investments in the quarter, mostly mark-to-market (paper) gains that can reverse — Shopify's own net income excluding equity investments was $594M.
