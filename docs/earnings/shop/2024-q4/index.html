Merchant solutions brings in $76.31 of every $100 — mostly a cut of merchants' sales — and subscription plans add $23.69. Direct costs take $51.92 and running the business takes another $31.54, leaving $16.54 of operating profit per $100. A $978M paper gain on equity investments added $34.78, and after a $22M equity-method loss and $128M of tax, Shopify kept $45.98 of every $100. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Growth accelerated into year-end

Revenue rose 31% to $2.81B as GMV grew 26% to $94.5B — the seventh straight quarter of 25% or greater revenue growth excluding logistics. Merchant solutions led, up 33% to $2.15B, while subscription revenue grew 27% to $666M and monthly recurring revenue reached $178M.

$465M

Operating profit jumped 61%

Operating income rose 61% to $465M from $289M a year ago, as gross profit grew 27% to $1.35B while operating expenses rose only 15%. Free cash flow was $611M — 22% of revenue, the highest margin of 2024, with the margin growing sequentially each quarter of the year.

$835M

Paper gains did the heavy lifting

Net income was $1.29B, but $835M of it (after tax) was a paper gain on equity investments — a $928M unrealized gain as the value of Shopify's stakes rose. Excluding equity investments, net income was $458M, up 36% from $337M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • An $835M after-tax paper gain on equity investments: a $928M unrealized gain on equity and other investments as the value of Shopify's stakes rose. Shopify says net income excluding the impact of equity investments was $458M (non-GAAP), versus $1,293M GAAP net income. The $22M loss on its equity-method investment (Flexport) is separate and already reflected in the GAAP figure.
