Merchant solutions brings in $73.73 of every $100 — mostly a cut of merchants' sales — but direct costs eat $50.47 of every $100 overall. Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+27%

Growth stayed strong

Revenue rose 27% to $2.36B as GMV grew 23% to $74.8B — the eighth straight quarter of pro forma revenue growth of 25% or more. Merchant solutions led, up 29% to $1.74B, while subscription revenue grew 21% to $620M and monthly recurring revenue reached $182M.

$203M

Operating profit more than doubled

Operating income rose 136% to $203M from $86M a year ago, as gross profit grew 22% to $1.17B while operating expenses rose only 11%. Free cash flow was $363M — 15% of revenue, the seventh straight quarter of double-digit free cash flow margins.

-$908M

Paper losses flipped the bottom line

Net loss was $682M, but $908M of it (after tax) was a paper loss on equity investments — a $930M unrealized loss on public-company stakes as share prices fell, plus an $86M unrealized loss on an investment option. Excluding equity investments, net income was $226M, up from $144M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $908M after-tax paper loss from equity investments: a $1,021M net loss on equity and other investments — a $930M unrealized loss on stakes in public companies as share prices fell, plus an $86M unrealized loss on an investment option — plus a $23M loss on its equity-method investment (Flexport). Shopify says net income excluding the impact of equity investments was $226M (non-GAAP), versus a $682M GAAP net loss.
