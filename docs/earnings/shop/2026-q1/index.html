Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Growth accelerated, GMV passed $100B

Revenue rose 34% to $3,170M as gross merchandise volume — everything merchants sold through Shopify — grew 35% to $100,743M, the first $100B quarter. Merchant solutions revenue rose 39% to $2,420M.

-$1,064M

A paper loss created the headline loss

Shopify's stakes in other companies fell in value by $1,064M on paper in the quarter. That non-cash mark-to-market loss sits below operating income, which was actually up 88% to $382M; excluding equity-investment effects, net income was $360M.

$476M

Cash flow stayed at a 15% margin

Free cash flow was $476M, 15% of revenue — the same margin as a year ago — while monthly recurring revenue reached $212M. For Q2, Shopify guided revenue growth in the high twenties percent.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Unrealized loss on equity and other investments: $1,064M — a non-cash mark-to-market loss on Shopify's stakes in other companies; Shopify excludes equity-investment effects from its own adjusted net income measure ($360M this quarter).
