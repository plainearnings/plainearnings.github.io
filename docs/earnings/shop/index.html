Merchant solutions brings in $77.66 of every $100 — mostly a cut of merchants' sales — but it is also the expensive part to run: direct costs eat $52.41 of every $100 overall.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+32%

Growth held at full speed

Revenue rose 32% to $3.74B, the same growth rate as a year ago but on a much bigger base. Merchant solutions led, up 35% to $2.91B, as $119.1B of sales flowed through the platform, up 29%.

22%

Cash generation is the quiet story

Free cash flow was $816M — 22% of revenue — from $818M of operating cash flow and just $2M of capital spending (capex — money spent on property and equipment).

-$127M

Investment losses trimmed the profit

Operating income jumped 66% to $568M, but net income was $462M: a $127M paper loss on equity investments, a $21M equity-method loss and $37M of tax all came out after the operating line.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $127M unrealized (mark-to-market — paper) loss on equity and other investments, partly offset by a $10M realized gain, plus a $21M loss on Shopify's equity-method investment, cut $138M from pre-tax profit. Shopify says net income excluding the impact of equity investments was $584M (non-GAAP).
