Merchant solutions brings in $75.52 of every $100 — mostly a cut of merchants' sales — but direct costs eat $51.42 of every $100 overall. Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+31%

Growth accelerated again

Revenue rose 31% to $2.68B, up from 21% growth a year ago, as GMV grew 31% to $87.8B. Growth accelerated in North America, Europe and Asia Pacific; Europe GMV grew 42% on a constant-currency basis. Merchant solutions led, up 37% to $2.02B, while subscription revenue grew 17% to $656M and monthly recurring revenue reached $185M.

$422M

Cash generation stayed strong

Free cash flow was $422M — 16% of revenue, the eighth straight quarter of double-digit free cash flow margins — from $428M of operating cash flow and just $6M of capital spending (capex — money spent on property and equipment). Operating income rose 21% to $291M.

+$568M

Paper gains inflated the bottom line

Net income jumped to $906M from $171M a year ago, but $568M of that (after tax) was a paper gain on equity investments — a $681M unrealized gain, versus a $79M paper loss a year ago — plus a $24M equity-method loss. Excluding equity investments, net income was $338M, up from $291M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $568M after-tax paper gain from equity investments: a $681M unrealized (paper) gain on equity and other investments and a $1M realized gain, partly offset by a $24M loss on its equity-method investment. Shopify says net income excluding the impact of equity investments was $338M (non-GAAP), versus $906M GAAP.
  • G&A looks like it doubled (+103% to $122M) only because a year ago Shopify released a $55M accrual for an estimated legal liability; on that basis underlying G&A rose about 6%. Shopify also booked a $10M impairment charge on right-of-use assets and leasehold improvements this quarter.
