Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+34%

Growth accelerated past 30% everywhere

Revenue rose 34% to $3,583M, GMV (sales through the platform) rose 32% to $115.6B, and merchant solutions revenue rose 37% to $2,781M as more sales ran through Shopify Payments.

$1.25B

Most of the profit is a paper gain

Net income was $1,502M, but $1,249M of it was an unrealized gain on equity and other investments — stakes rising in value, not cash from the business. Shopify's own measure excluding equity investments puts net income at $439M.

$654M

The core business still prints cash

Free cash flow was $654M — 18% of revenue — and operating income rose 68% to $488M. For Q3 Shopify guided revenue growth in the low thirties, percent-wise.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • The $1,249M unrealized gain on equity and other investments included $731M from stakes with market prices and $522M of gross gains from an observable price change on a private investment, less an $11M loss on an investment option (10-Q detail). These paper gains are why GAAP net income of $1,502M is more than three times Shopify's $439M figure excluding equity investments.
