Subscription plans bring in $27.46 of every $100 and merchant solutions $72.54 — mostly a cut of merchants' sales. Direct costs take $48.58 and running the business takes another $46.80, leaving $4.62 of operating profit per $100. But a $373M paper loss on investments and a $44M loss on the Flexport stake overwhelmed it: after $17M of tax, Shopify lost $14.67 of every $100. Rows may not add exactly due to rounding.

Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+23%

Growth stayed strong

Revenue rose 23% to $1.86B as GMV grew 23% to $60.9B — 29% after adjusting for the sale of the logistics businesses. Subscription revenue grew 34% to $511M on more merchants and higher plan prices, with monthly recurring revenue up 32% to $151M, while merchant solutions rose 20% to $1.35B as Shopify Payments processed 60% of GMV ($36.2B).

$86M

Operating profit returned

Operating income was $86M (5% margin) versus a $193M loss a year ago. On an adjusted basis, operating income was $201M (11% margin) versus an adjusted loss of $31M, and gross margin widened to 51.4% from 47.5% as the dilutive logistics business exited. Free cash flow more than doubled to $232M, a 12% margin versus 6% a year ago.

-$273M

Investment losses turned profit into a net loss

Net income was a $273M loss despite the operating profit: a $373M unrealized loss on equity investments — mostly a $351M drop in listed holdings as share prices fell — plus a $44M loss on the Flexport stake, partly offset by $79M of interest income. Excluding equity investments, adjusted net income was $256M versus $12M a year ago.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $373M unrealized loss on equity and other investments — mostly a $351M drop in the value of investments with readily determinable fair values as their share prices fell during the quarter (including a $16M unrealized loss on Shopify's option to buy Klaviyo shares). Excluding equity investments, adjusted net income was $256M (non-GAAP) versus a $273M GAAP net loss.
  • $44M net loss on Shopify's equity-method investment in Flexport.
  • Stock-based compensation of $105M — a non-cash expense inside operating expenses ($68M in R&D, $25M in G&A, $11M in sales and marketing, plus $1M in cost of revenues). It is recurring, not one-off, but it is paid in shares rather than cash, so it does not reduce the $232M of free cash flow Shopify generated.
