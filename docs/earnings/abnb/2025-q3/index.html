Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

133.6M

Bookings accelerated

Nights and seats booked grew 9% — faster than last quarter — and gross booking value rose 14% to $22.9B as the average daily rate climbed 5% to $171. Growth was double-digit outside North America, and the new Reserve Now, Pay Later option helped bookings in the U.S.

$2.1B

Record operating profit

Adjusted EBITDA topped $2.0B for the first time in any quarter, a 50% margin. Airbnb collects guest money before the stay and owns no hotels, so growth converts unusually cleanly into profit and $1.3B of free cash flow.

$213M

A one-off tax charge held profit flat

Net income of $1,374M was nearly unchanged from $1,368M a year ago. The tax provision included a $213M valuation allowance against tax credits related to the Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax — without it, net income would have grown.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • $213M valuation allowance against deferred tax assets related to Corporate Alternative Minimum Tax (CAMT) credits, recorded inside the $418M tax provision. The shareholder letter calls it out as the reason net income was flat year over year despite 10% revenue growth.
