Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

The industrial recovery showed up in every segment

Revenue grew 16% to $4.45B and 9% sequentially, led by a continued broad recovery in industrial demand. Analog, the biggest business, grew 18% to $3.45B; Embedded Processing grew 10% and Other grew 14% — growth across every segment.

35.1%

Sales grew three times faster than spending

Operating profit rose 25% to $1.56B, a 35.1% operating margin, on gross profit of $2.58B. R&D and SG&A together were $1.01B, up just 5% while revenue grew 16% — the extra sales fell mostly to the bottom line. Net income rose 15% to $1.30B and EPS rose 16% to $1.41.

$6.7B

Big payouts, bigger factory bill

Over the past 12 months TI returned $6.71B to owners — $4.90B in dividends and $1.81B in buybacks — while investing $4.94B in new factories. Free cash flow for the same period was $1.76B, just 10.6% of revenue, because that factory spending is so heavy. Q3 guidance is $4.45–$4.80B of revenue and EPS of $1.36–$1.60.
