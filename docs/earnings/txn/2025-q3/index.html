Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+16%

Analog did the lifting

Analog revenue rose 16% to $3.73B — 79% of all sales — while Embedded Processing grew 9% to $709M and Other rose 11% to $304M.

57.4%

Margins eased

Gross margin slipped to 57.4% from 59.6% a year ago, but the revenue growth still lifted operating profit 7% to $1.66B.

$1,355M

Cash returned to owners

TI returned $1,355M in the quarter — $1,236M of dividends and $119M of buybacks — against $1,068M of free cash flow.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Earnings per share included a 10-cent reduction that was not in the company's original guidance, as stated in the release.
  • The $85M of restructuring charges/other is reported inside the 'Other' segment, whose operating profit fell to $69M from $129M a year ago.
