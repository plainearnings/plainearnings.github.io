Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+11%

Sales grew in nearly every market

Revenue grew 11% to $4.07B and 2% sequentially, with every market growing sequentially except a seasonal decline in personal electronics. Analog, the biggest business, grew 13% to $3.21B; Other grew 23% and Embedded Processing slipped 1%.

32.5%

Profit grew slower than sales

Operating profit rose only 3% to $1.32B, a 32.5% operating margin, down from 35.1% a year ago. Gross margin narrowed to 56.8% and R&D plus SG&A together were $989M, up 6% while revenue grew 11%. Net income rose 7% to $1.18B and EPS rose 7% to $1.28, helped by a $69M discrete tax benefit.

$1.72B

Cash is tight while factories get built

Free cash flow for the trailing 12 months was $1.72B, 10.7% of revenue, but just -$14M in the quarter itself after $1.12B of capital spending on new factories. TI still returned $1.89B to owners in the quarter — $1.24B in dividends and $653M in buybacks. Q2 guidance is $4.17–$4.53B of revenue and EPS of $1.21–$1.47.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $69M discrete tax benefit (about 5 cents of EPS) that was not in the company's original guidance — operating taxes were $166M, less $69M of discrete items, for a $97M provision.
  • The year-ago quarter included a $124M restructuring/other credit, largely a gain on asset sales, which did not repeat — that is most of why the Other segment's operating profit fell 55% to $78M even as its revenue grew 23%.
