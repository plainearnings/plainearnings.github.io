Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+63%

Operating leverage is the story, not revenue

Revenue grew a solid 12%, but operating income grew 63% because R&D and G&A both fell in absolute terms after the May 2026 restructuring. The 2026 Plan is substantially complete, so this cost base is roughly the new normal — the test now is whether Intuit can keep growing revenue at double digits without spending ramping back up.

$3.68B

Mailchimp is leaving at a steep loss — and that's the point

Intuit paid $12 billion for Mailchimp in 2021 and is selling it for $3.68 billion — $3.31 billion in cash plus $368 million in Bending Spoons stock. But Mailchimp's revenue is flat-to-down and it no longer fits the AI-platform strategy; the cash will fund buybacks instead. The sale closes in early calendar 2027.

$199M

Q1 is the quiet quarter — the real test is tax season

TurboTax did just $199M this quarter; the January–April season is where Intuit's year is made. Credit Karma's guidance was trimmed for the full year, which is why total revenue guidance came down even as profit guidance was reiterated. What matters now: Q2 guidance ($4.93–4.95B revenue, +6–7%) and whether the tax season lands.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $118M net gain on long-term investments sits inside interest and other income — it more than doubled that line to $170M and added about $0.44 per share to GAAP EPS. Non-GAAP net income ($780M) excludes it; it is a market move, not operating performance.
  • An $11M restructuring charge from the May 2026 reorganization plan (total expected cost about $315M; actions substantially complete this quarter). Excluded from non-GAAP results.
  • Tax shortfalls of $35M on share-based compensation pushed the GAAP effective tax rate to 28.0%, versus 20.5% a year ago when Intuit recognized $30M of excess tax benefits. Excluding discrete items, the rate was about 24% in both years.
  • The Mailchimp sale was announced, not closed — no gain or loss is in this quarter's numbers. The 10-Q says Intuit expects to recognize a pre-tax gain at close, depending on Mailchimp's carrying value and the closing-date value of the Bending Spoons stock.
  • With this release Intuit trimmed full-year FY27 revenue guidance to $23,081M–$23,365M (+8–9%) on a Credit Karma change, but reiterated profit guidance: GAAP operating income $7,408M–$7,490M and GAAP EPS $20.12–$20.36. Q2 FY27 guidance: revenue $4,931M–$4,954M (+6–7%).
