Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$221M

Record new business

Net new ARR — new subscription value signed in the quarter — hit a record $221.1M, taking ending ARR to $4.66B, up 20% year over year. Over 1,000 customers are now on the Flex subscription model.

-$113M

In the red on paper

The GAAP operating loss was $113.0M as $284.2M of stock-based pay weighed, plus $35.7M of July 19 Incident costs and $38.4M of strategic-plan charges; non-GAAP operating income was a record $255.0M.

$284M

Cash flow tells another story

Free cash flow was a Q2-record $283.6M on $332.8M of operating cash flow, and cash grew to a record $4.97B — CrowdStrike's cash generation is far stronger than its GAAP result suggests.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Costs tied to the July 19 Incident and related matters were $35.7M in the quarter, vs $5.1M a year ago.
  • Charges tied to the May 2025 strategic plan — cutting about 500 roles, 5% of the workforce — were $38.4M, mostly severance ($20.1M) and stock pay ($17.9M).
  • Stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes were $284.2M — the main reason non-GAAP net income attributable to CrowdStrike ($237.4M) contrasts with the GAAP attributable net loss ($77.7M).
