Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$3.11B

Subscriptions did the growing

Subscription revenue grew 22.5% to $3.11B — 97% of all revenue — with 89 deals over $1M in net new annual contract value in the quarter. The customer base keeps moving upmarket: 528 customers now spend more than $5M a year, up about 19.5%, and customers over $20M grew more than 30%.

$10.92B

The backlog is growing faster than sales

Current remaining performance obligations — revenue already contracted for the next 12 months — grew 24.5% to $10.92B, and total remaining obligations reached $23.9B, up 29%. The contracted backlog is growing faster than revenue, which is what funds the next quarters.

$955M

The GAAP profit is a third of the adjusted one

GAAP operating income was just $358M, an 11.1% margin, while non-GAAP operating income was $955M at a 29.5% margin. The $597M gap is mostly $499M of stock-based pay, plus a $30M asset impairment and $29M of severance. Free cash flow was $535M, a 16.5% margin, and ServiceNow bought back $361M of its own stock.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $30M impairment of assets in the quarter — a write-down excluded from non-GAAP results; there was none a year ago.
  • Severance costs of $29M in the quarter, excluded from non-GAAP results; there were none a year ago.
  • Stock-based compensation of $499M (up from $444M a year ago) is the largest non-GAAP add-back and the main reason GAAP operating income of $358M trails non-GAAP operating income of $955M. It is excluded from non-GAAP results, but it is a real cost — it dilutes existing shareholders.
