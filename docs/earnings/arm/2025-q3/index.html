Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$983M

Record revenue on record royalties

Revenue rose 19% to a record $983M, above the high end of guidance, on record royalty revenue and better-than-expected licensing. License revenue rose 14% to $403M and royalty revenue rose 23% to a record $580M. For the first nine months of the fiscal year, revenue was $2,766M, up 20% year over year.

+23%

Royalties keep climbing on Armv9 and CSS

Royalty revenue grew 23% to $580M on wider use of the Armv9 architecture, the ramp of Arm CSS ready-made compute subsystems, IoT improvement, and more Arm-based chips in data centers. CSS is now shipping in volume and materially contributing to royalty growth. AWS said more than 50% of new CPU capacity installed over the past two years was on Graviton, and over 90% of its top 1,000 EC2 customers use Graviton.

$252M

Net income nearly tripled

GAAP net income nearly tripled to $252M, up 190% from $87M a year ago, or $0.24 per share versus $0.08. Operating income was $175M, up 31%, even with record R&D investment of $533M. A $39M gain on equity investments and $26M of other non-operating income helped, and tax was just 6.0%. Excluding the equity gain and share-based pay, non-GAAP net income was $417M, or $0.39 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Arm recorded a $39M gain from equity investments in the quarter, which it strips out of its non-GAAP results: excluding it (and share-based pay), non-GAAP net income was $417M versus $252M GAAP.
