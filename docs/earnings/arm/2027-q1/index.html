Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+22%

Both halves of the business grew over 20%

License revenue rose 23% to $574M and royalty revenue 22% to $715M — a record first quarter. Data center royalties more than doubled as Arm-based server chips spread, and Neoverse shipments have now passed 1.5 billion cores.

$0.25

GAAP profit is much smaller than the headline

GAAP EPS was $0.25 while non-GAAP EPS was $0.45. The gap is mostly $343M of share-based pay plus $90M of employer taxes — and $128M of net income came from fair-value gains on listed-company investments, not operations.

$1.38B

Next quarter is guided higher

Arm guided Q2 FY27 revenue to $1.38B (plus or minus $50M) with non-GAAP EPS of $0.47 (plus or minus $0.04). Demand for its new Arm AGI CPU now exceeds $2B across fiscal 2027–28, double the $1B opportunity outlined last quarter.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Share-based pay is the main gap between the two profit numbers: the non-GAAP reconciliation adds back $343M of equity-settled share-based compensation plus $90M of related employer taxes (net of R&D incentives) and $7M of disposal and restructuring costs, which is why GAAP net income of $270M sits far below non-GAAP net income of $480M.
  • Net income got a $128M lift from income on equity investments — mostly $126M of fair-value gains on shares in publicly listed companies. That is market-driven, not operating profit, and Arm excludes it from its non-GAAP results.
