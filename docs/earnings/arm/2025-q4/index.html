Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

$1,241M

First billion-dollar quarter

Revenue rose 34% to a record $1,241M — the first time Arm has crossed $1B in a quarter. License revenue jumped 53% to a record $634M and royalty revenue rose 18% to a record $607M. For the full fiscal year, revenue was a record $4,007M, up 24%, with royalty revenue above $2B for the first time.

+18%

Royalties keep climbing on Armv9 and CSS

Royalty revenue grew 18% to $607M on wider use of the Armv9 architecture, the ramp of Arm CSS ready-made compute subsystems, and more Arm-based chips in data centers. Smartphone royalties grew about 30% even as phone shipments rose under 2% — Arm is earning more per chip.

$290M

An equity-investment loss shrank the bottom line

GAAP operating income was $410M, up from $22M a year ago, but a $290M loss from equity investments cut pre-tax income to $144M. A $66M tax benefit lifted GAAP net income to $210M, or $0.20 per share; excluding the equity loss and share-based pay, non-GAAP net income was $584M, or $0.55 per share.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Arm recorded a $290M loss from equity investments in the quarter, which it strips out of its non-GAAP results: excluding it (and share-based pay), non-GAAP net income was $584M versus $210M GAAP.
