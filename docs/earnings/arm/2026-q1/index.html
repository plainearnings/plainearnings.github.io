Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+25%

Royalties set a first-quarter record

Royalty revenue rose 25% to $585M, a record for a first quarter, while license revenue eased 1% to $468M — total revenue of $1,053M was Arm's second billion-dollar quarter in a row.

$650M

Record R&D spending squeezed operating profit

GAAP research and development jumped to $650M from $485M a year ago as engineering headcount grew, so operating income fell to $114M from $182M despite the record revenue.

$0.12

GAAP profit fell; the adjusted picture was steadier

GAAP EPS was $0.12, down from $0.21 a year ago, while non-GAAP EPS was $0.35. Arm generated $332M of operating cash flow and $150M of non-GAAP free cash flow.
