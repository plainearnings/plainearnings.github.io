Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+50%

Licensing is doing the heavy lifting

License and other revenue jumped 50% to $773M — 54% of all revenue — as customers paid up for newer Arm technology. Those designs enter production later, so today's licensing surge points to future royalty growth.

$0.12

GAAP profit is much smaller than it looks

GAAP EPS was $0.12, down from $0.22 a year ago, while non-GAAP EPS rose to $0.52. The gap is mostly $377M of share-based pay counted under GAAP — and last year's GAAP figure was flattered by a $131M gain on selling a business.

$1.54B

Next quarter is guided higher

Arm guided Q3 FY27 revenue to $1.54B (plus or minus $50M) with non-GAAP EPS of $0.56 (plus or minus $0.04). Data center royalties already doubled this quarter as Arm-based server chips spread through the big cloud providers.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Share-based pay is the main gap between the two profit numbers: the non-GAAP reconciliation adds back $377M of equity-settled share-based compensation (plus $48M of related employer taxes, net, and $12M of acquisition-related expenses), which is why GAAP net income of $130M sits far below non-GAAP net income of $566M.
  • The year-ago quarter included a $131M gain on selling a business, inside its $128M of other non-operating income — this quarter had no such gain, which is a big reason GAAP net income fell 45% YoY even as revenue rose 27%.
