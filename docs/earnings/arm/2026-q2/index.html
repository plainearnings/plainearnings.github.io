Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+21%

Royalties set a record

Royalty revenue rose 21% to a record $620M and license revenue jumped 56% to $515M, taking total revenue to $1,135M — Arm's third straight billion-dollar quarter, above the high end of guidance.

$131M

A one-off gain flattered the bottom line

GAAP net income more than doubled to $238M, but $131M of that came from a one-time gain on selling a business, booked in other income. Excluding one-offs and share-based pay, non-GAAP net income was $417M (EPS $0.39).

$411M

Cash generation was strong

Operating cash flow was $567M and non-GAAP free cash flow $411M in the quarter. Annualized contract value rose 28% to $1,600M, while remaining performance obligations were $2,246M.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • Gain on business divestiture: $131M inside other non-operating income — a one-time gain on a sale; Arm excludes it from non-GAAP results.
