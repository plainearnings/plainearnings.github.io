Three things to take away

If you only remember three numbers from this quarter, make it these.

+52%

Lending is now the growth engine

Platform-related revenue rose 52% to $273.6M. MyPay originations hit a record $5.3B with about $80M of transaction profit, and Instant Loans — used by more than 20% of members — originated $381M. Payments revenue still grew a solid 22% to $444.4M.

10.9M

Members are piling in

Active Members grew 20% to 10.9 million, with a record 1.8 million net adds over the past year and about 500,000 in the quarter alone. Average revenue per member rose 10% to $270, and purchase volume grew 19% to $38.5B.

3rd

Profit is real, but still thin

This was Chime's third consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability: $43.7M of net income, a 6% margin, and adjusted EBITDA of $137M, a 19% margin. A $17.1M restructuring charge and $76.3M of stock-based pay sit inside the numbers. Chime raised full-year revenue guidance to $2.79–2.80B.

One-time items to know about

Things that make this quarter look different from a normal one — so you don't mistake them for the trend.

  • A $17.1M restructuring charge for employee severance and related costs sits inside operating expenses (Technology $8.6M, G&A $4.7M, Member support $2.0M, Sales & marketing $1.7M). Excluding it and stock-based pay, adjusted EBITDA was $137.3M.
  • Stock-based compensation and related payroll tax was $76.3M in the quarter — a real cost that GAAP counts and adjusted EBITDA sets aside.
