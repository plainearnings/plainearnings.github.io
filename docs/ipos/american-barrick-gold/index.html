American Barrick Gold Corporation ABGC

Filed · awaiting date

American Barrick Gold holds Barrick Mining's North American gold mines, including Nevada Gold Mines — the world's largest gold-mining complex — and the Pueblo Viejo mine in the Dominican Republic. It digs gold out of the ground and sells it.

The story so far

Barrick Mining, one of the world's biggest gold miners, is spinning off its North American gold business into a separate listed company. The new company filed its S-1 on Nov 10, 2026, with $11.4B of revenue in 2025; Barrick will stay in control after the offering and plans to return the IPO proceeds to its own shareholders. Reuters called it one of the most anticipated public offerings among gold miners in recent years.

Why it's on the list

  • Renaissance Capital estimates it could raise ~$6B — a multibillion-dollar deal, rare for a miner
  • Holds Nevada Gold Mines, the world's largest gold-mining complex, plus the Pueblo Viejo mine in the Dominican Republic
  • Barrick stays in control after the listing — a partial spin-out, not a full exit
  • No price range or share count yet — terms come once the SEC review advances
