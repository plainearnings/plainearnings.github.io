Aggreko Inc. AGKO

Filed · awaiting date

Aggreko is the world's largest renter of temporary power — generators, batteries and cooling that keep festivals, mines, factories and whole towns running when the grid can't.

The story so far

Founded in 1962 in Scotland, Aggreko was listed in London for decades before being taken private in 2021. Its Aug 24, 2026 filing sets up a return to public markets, this time in the US. No terms yet.

Why it's on the list

  • A well-known, profitable business rather than a startup — a different flavour of IPO
  • Hurricane and grid-emergency work makes it quietly newsworthy most years
