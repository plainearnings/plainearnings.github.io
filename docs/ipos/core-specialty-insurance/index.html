Core Specialty Insurance Holdings, Inc. CORE

Filed · awaiting date

Core Specialty is a US specialty insurer — it covers property and casualty risks that standard insurers often won't touch, selling through brokers and specialist underwriting teams rather than directly to the public.

The story so far

Founded in 2020 in Cincinnati, Core Specialty grew fast by buying and building specialty insurance businesses — gross premiums written rose from $457M in 2020 to $4.57B in 2025. It filed its S-1 on Nov 16, 2026; unusually, all the shares on offer come from existing stockholders, so the company itself receives no money from the IPO. J.P. Morgan and Goldman Sachs are leading the deal.

Why it's on the list

  • A rare all-secondary offering: existing owners are selling, the company gets no proceeds
  • Profitable and growing: $84.9M net income in the first nine months of 2026, with a 93.0% combined ratio
  • Gross premiums of $4.57B in 2025 make this one of the larger insurer IPOs in the pipeline
  • No price range or share count yet — terms come once the SEC review advances
