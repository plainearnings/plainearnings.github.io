The story so far

Founded in 2013 in Finland, Oura created the smart-ring category and has sold millions of rings; it is one of the best-known consumer health-tech names to file in 2026.

Why it's on the list

  • Postponed its planned Sep 30 listing on Sep 29, 2026, citing uncertainty in the IPO market — despite reported ~4× oversubscription; no new date announced
  • At ~$2.1B, one of the biggest consumer-tech deals in the current pipeline
  • Terms were already set in the filing ($40–$44), so a date could come quickly
