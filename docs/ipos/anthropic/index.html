Anthropic, PBC ANT

Scheduled · Thu, Nov 19, 2026

Anthropic builds Claude, a family of AI models used by businesses, developers and everyday users for writing, coding and analysis. It sells access to its models and subscriptions, and counts Google and Amazon among its biggest backers.

The story so far

Founded in 2021 in San Francisco by former OpenAI researchers and led by CEO Dario Amodei, Anthropic is set up as a public benefit corporation. It reported revenue of about $33B in the first nine months of 2026, up from $2.5B a year earlier. This is set to be the second-largest IPO in history, after SpaceX in June 2026.

Why it's on the list

  • Second-largest IPO ever, behind only SpaceX (June 2026)
  • Reported about 6× oversubscribed (Bloomberg), with an unusually large ~20% of the deal aimed at retail investors
  • Seven of its suppliers — Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC — agreed to buy $26B of stock alongside the IPO
  • Amazon will hold over 5% of the company after the offering
