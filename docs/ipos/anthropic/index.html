Now trading · listed Thu, Nov 19, 2026

The story so far

Founded in 2021 in San Francisco by former OpenAI researchers and led by CEO Dario Amodei, Anthropic is set up as a public benefit corporation. It reported revenue of about $33B in the first nine months of 2026, up from $2.5B a year earlier. It priced its IPO at $83 a share on Nov 18, 2026 — above the $70–$78 range — and began trading on Nasdaq on Nov 19. Underwriters then fully exercised their option for 147M extra shares, taking the offering to 1,127M shares and about $93.5B gross proceeds; with the $26B private placement to seven suppliers, the combined raise was about $119.5B. At $81.3B before the greenshoe, it was already the largest IPO ever by proceeds.

Why it's on the list

  • Priced at $83 on Nov 18, above the $70–$78 range, and upsized from 825M to 980M shares — the largest IPO ever by proceeds
  • Underwriters fully exercised their option for 147M extra shares, lifting the offering to 1,127M shares and ~$93.5B gross proceeds (company announcement, Nov 19)
  • Market data (Reuters/WSJ): shares fell 8.5% on the second day, Fri Nov 20, closing at $88.91 — still 7% above the $83 IPO price, after briefly touching $83.05 in a broad AI sell-off
  • Market data (Reuters, Nov 22): five brokerages outside the underwriting syndicate initiated coverage after the debut, with price targets averaging $134 (range $105–$155); the deal's banks cannot publish research until the quiet period ends around Nov 30
  • Market data (Reuters): shares opened at $111.50 and closed the first day at $97.17, up 17% on the $83 IPO price
  • Reported about 6× oversubscribed (Bloomberg), with an unusually large ~20% of the deal aimed at retail investors
  • Seven of its suppliers — Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC — agreed to buy $26B of stock alongside the IPO
  • Amazon will hold over 5% of the company after the offering
