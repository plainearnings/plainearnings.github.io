Now trading · listed Thu, Nov 19, 2026

The story so far

Founded in 2021 in San Francisco by former OpenAI researchers and led by CEO Dario Amodei, Anthropic is set up as a public benefit corporation. It reported revenue of about $33B in the first nine months of 2026, up from $2.5B a year earlier. It priced its IPO at $83 a share on Nov 18, 2026 — above the $70–$78 range — and began trading on Nasdaq on Nov 19. Underwriters then fully exercised their option for 147M extra shares, taking the offering to 1,127M shares and about $93.5B gross proceeds; with the $26B private placement to seven suppliers, the combined raise was about $119.5B. At $81.3B before the greenshoe, it was already the largest IPO ever by proceeds.

Why it's on the list

  • TeraWulf 8-K (Nov 4): Google agreed to backstop Anthropic's 20-year lease for ~401 MW at TeraWulf's Kentucky data-centre campus — about $19B of contracted rent over the term, with capacity delivered from late 2027; the lease itself was signed in July 2026
  • Prospectus lockups (reported Nov 26): about 1.3B shares become eligible to trade by Feb 2, 2027, with up to 1.7B more unlocked after 2026 results — the tradable float could roughly triple
  • Market data (reported Nov 26): Anthropic is in line for additions to major stock indexes, including the Nasdaq-100, though its small free float is expected to keep initial weights low
  • Market data (Finnhub / Investor's Business Daily, Nov 25): shares closed at $82.79 on Wed Nov 25, up 1.1% on the day — a second straight close below the $83 IPO price — after plunging to an intraday low of $73.85 in a morning sell-off before recovering
  • 8-K (filed Nov 25): the IPO closed Nov 20 with 1,127M Class A shares sold at $83 — 1,103,351,354 by the company (~$91.6B net to Anthropic) and 23,648,646 by the seven co-founders — plus the $26B private placement; Anthropic also sold one Class F share to Founder LLC for $7.00, carrying 50.1% of total voting power
  • Market data (TipRanks / TradingView, Nov 26): ARK Invest bought 751,710 shares on Nov 24 (~$62M) and 317,053 more on Nov 25 (~$26.2M) — about $88M in two days — taking its stake to ~2.4M shares (~$196M); Anthropic joins MSCI indexes at the close on Nov 30
  • Market data (Reuters, Nov 24): shares closed at $81.91 on Tue Nov 24, down 6.5% on the day — the first close below the $83 IPO price, after falling in every session since the Nov 19 debut
  • Form 4 filings (Nov 23): CEO Dario Amodei and president Daniela Amodei each sold 3,378,378 shares at the $83 IPO price on Nov 20 — about $280M each, ~$561M combined; all seven co-founders sold identical blocks totalling ~$1.96B, as set out in the prospectus
  • Launched Claude Ship on Nov 23 — AI agents that review, fix and approve code pull requests — its first major product launch since listing
  • Priced at $83 on Nov 18, above the $70–$78 range, and upsized from 825M to 980M shares — the largest IPO ever by proceeds
  • Underwriters fully exercised their option for 147M extra shares, lifting the offering to 1,127M shares and ~$93.5B gross proceeds (company announcement, Nov 19)
  • Market data (Reuters/WSJ): shares fell 8.5% on the second day, Fri Nov 20, closing at $88.91 — still 7% above the $83 IPO price, after briefly touching $83.05 in a broad AI sell-off
  • Market data (Reuters, Nov 22): five brokerages outside the underwriting syndicate initiated coverage after the debut, with price targets averaging $134 (range $105–$155); the deal's banks cannot publish research until the quiet period ends around Nov 30
  • Market data (Reuters): shares opened at $111.50 and closed the first day at $97.17, up 17% on the $83 IPO price
  • Reported about 6× oversubscribed (Bloomberg), with an unusually large ~20% of the deal aimed at retail investors
  • Seven of its suppliers — Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC — agreed to buy $26B of stock alongside the IPO
  • Amazon will hold over 5% of the company after the offering
  • Market data (Motley Fool, Nov 23): shares closed at $87.64 on Mon Nov 23, down 1.4% on the day — still above the $83 IPO price — even as the Nasdaq posted its best day since April 2025
